# Therapy Diary

An iPhone app for writing down what happened and how it felt, in separate diaries.

A hard moment is easier to look at later when the note is already split into pieces: the feeling you walked in with, what happened, the feeling at the time, the thought that showed up, and a free note. Each diary is its own shelf, so one year does not get mixed with another.

## What you can do

- Open the app on a short logo, then a home screen of your diaries.
- See the two diaries you updated most recently, and the full list under them.
- Open a diary and read its entries, newest dates included in the list.
- Open an entry in Overview or Writing.
- In Overview, pick emotions, jump into a writing section, and set an intensity from 0 to 10.
- In Writing, switch between what happened, the thought, and a free note.
- Search the emotion list and confirm the ones you want.

The diaries and entries you see are sample data built into the app. Search, add, and the more button on the home screen are on screen and do not do anything yet. Nothing is saved to an account or a server, and changes are gone when you leave the screen.

## How to run

1. Install Xcode from the Mac App Store.
2. Open `therapy-App/therapy-App.xcodeproj`.
3. Choose an iPhone simulator in the toolbar.
4. Press Run (⌘R).

The logo stays up for about 3 seconds, then the home screen appears.
